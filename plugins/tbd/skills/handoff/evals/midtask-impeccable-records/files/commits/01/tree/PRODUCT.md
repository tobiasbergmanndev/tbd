# Product

## Users

Busy young plant owners who forget to water. They open the app for a few seconds, see what
needs water today, tap "watered", and close it.

## Purpose

Keep house plants alive without thinking about it: one glance shows which plant needs water
now.

## Voice

Gentle and brief. Never nagging, never guilt ("Your fern is thirsty", not "You forgot your
fern again!").

## Constraints

- Offline first: works without a connection, installs as a PWA.
- No account, no server: everything stays on the device.

## Platform

web
